# IsoproTech Roof Recovery Mode

Apply this overlay together with the normal weekly SEO policy and fast-growth overlay.

Current business priority: roof-work visibility is too weak relative to competitors, so increase execution intensity without lowering quality.

## Operating cadence
- The SEO engine runs twice per week.
- At least one sprint per week must be a roof cluster sprint while roof visibility remains weak.
- The second sprint may choose the strongest near-win across roof or facade, but roof wins ties.
- Do not repeat the same micro-fix across two runs.

## Roof-first prioritization
Prioritize commercially valuable roof queries with live evidence, especially:
- dak isoleren
- dakisolatie
- dakisolatie Antwerpen
- plat dak isoleren / plat dak isolatie
- hellend dak isoleren
- sarkingdak / sarking isolatie / PIR dakisolatie
- dakrenovatie isolatie

Choose the fastest credible path to Top 5:
1. near-win roof queries around positions 4-20,
2. correct existing roof page with clear intent match,
3. contextual internal-link authority from existing relevant pages,
4. content-depth / intent coverage gaps,
5. only then distant roof terms unless their lead value is materially higher.

## Stronger sprint standard
When safe and evidence-backed, combine related actions in one sprint instead of one cosmetic edit:
- on-page relevance,
- title/H1/snippet alignment,
- contextual internal links,
- content coverage needed for the same intent,
- cannibalization cleanup that is already safe under policy.

If the real bottleneck is authority/off-page, say so explicitly rather than endlessly editing copy.

## Measurement
Every roof sprint must report:
- current Top 5 / Top 10 / Top 20 roof-query counts,
- the exact roof queries targeted,
- current GSC position and impressions,
- what changed,
- the next checkpoint,
- whether the ranking bottleneck is on-page, internal authority, cannibalization, technical, or off-page.

This overlay does not override approval rules, factual-claim safety, technical-health checks, or the ban on invented results.
