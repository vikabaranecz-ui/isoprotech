# IsoproTech Full-Funnel SEO + Conversion Plan

Use this overlay together with the normal weekly SEO policy, fast-growth mode and roof-recovery mode.

## Primary business outcome
The objective is more qualified organic enquiries and more completed forms for IsoproTech's verified insulation, roof and facade services. Rankings and traffic are leading indicators, not the final goal.

Weekly work must optimize for:
1. qualified roof/facade/insulation enquiries,
2. completed contact/offerte forms,
3. visibility across high-intent and pre-purchase searches,
4. useful content that builds trust and helps a homeowner decide,
5. technically healthy pages that convert on mobile and desktop.

Never invent conversion numbers when analytics do not provide them.

## Whole-market coverage map
Every run must review the broader demand map, not only the current two keyword phrases.

Maintain active coverage across:
- roof commercial intent: dakisolatie, dak isoleren, plat dak, hellend dak, sarking/PIR, dakrenovatie + isolatie;
- facade commercial intent: gevelisolatie, crepi, spuitkurk, steenstrips where verified;
- local intent: Antwerpen/service-area combinations supported by business facts;
- problem intent: cold house, cold walls, heat loss, overheating upstairs/attic, rapid cooling;
- economic/decision intent: what to insulate first, comparing solutions, renovation planning, current subsidy/rule questions only when verified;
- trust intent: process, materials already verified, project examples, what happens during a visit/quote, how work is coordinated;
- seasonal intent: winter heat-loss and summer overheating topics prepared before demand peaks.

For every material gap decide:
- existing page -> strengthen it;
- cannibalization -> resolve;
- weak internal authority -> reinforce;
- distinct intent with demand -> prepare NEEDS APPROVAL new page/article;
- weak relevance -> do not create content.

## Weekly sprint structure
A strong sprint normally contains:
1. one primary commercial ranking move,
2. one conversion/trust improvement on the same journey when justified,
3. one supporting internal-link/content-gap action when safe.

Roof recovery still requires at least one roof-focused sprint per week while roof visibility is weak.

## Conversion-first content standard
SEO copy must help a real homeowner understand the problem and make a decision. It must not read like filler for search engines.

Where relevant and verified, commercial pages should:
- explain who the service is for and what problem it solves;
- explain the practical process in clear Dutch;
- answer serious buyer questions before they request a quote;
- distinguish options such as roof/facade insulation methods in customer language;
- use verified project/material facts where they add trust;
- explain the next step after contacting IsoproTech;
- include a clear natural CTA;
- avoid keyword stuffing, generic filler and repeated city/service phrases.

Useful, specific copy is preferred over longer copy.

## Form-conversion review
For the selected journey, review:
- CTA wording/placement,
- whether the visitor understands what happens after form submission,
- trust/proof near the CTA,
- mobile friction,
- whether the page answers enough questions to justify requesting a visit/quote.

Only make changes automatically when allowed by the existing policy. Larger form/UX changes are approval-gated.

## Gap register
Every weekly issue must include a compact "Coverage gaps" section:
- highest-value unfilled roof gap,
- highest-value unfilled facade gap,
- highest-value problem/decision gap,
- highest-value conversion/trust gap,
- status for each: EXECUTE NOW, NEEDS APPROVAL, MONITOR, or NOT RELEVANT.

## Quality bar
Do not publish low-value text for word count.
Do not create near-duplicate pages.
Do not invent prices, savings, guarantees, subsidy amounts, timelines, reviews, service areas or technical claims.
Every change must have a ranking hypothesis, conversion hypothesis, or both.
